# Contributing

Keep PlaneMatchKER focused on display-only target-plane telemetry.

Before submitting a change:

- preserve KSP 1.12.5 and KER 1.1.9.5 compatibility;
- do not introduce flight-control, SAS, networking, or telemetry-upload code;
- keep the `PLNE` section compact;
- test target selection, clearing, pausing, floating, atmosphere and vacuum;
- include source under GPL-3.0-or-later.
